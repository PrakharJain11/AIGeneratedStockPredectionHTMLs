EBITDA margin is expected to structurally expand from the historical ~10.4% base toward 12.49% in FY28E and 13.20% in FY29E. Secured by lead price softening, CML recycling self-sufficiency, and EESL operating leverage.

PAT growth (+57.09% in FY27E) is mathematically faster than EBITDA growth (+30.65% in FY27E). Because Exide carries zero net debt, zero interest burden dilutes operating cash flow down to PBT, offset only by depreciation from EESL plant capitalization.

1. Post-pandemic automotive vehicle replacement cycle hitting primary 2.5-3 yr window.

8. What could cause the forecast to fail? +

Execution delays at the EESL plant. If 6 GWh Phase 1 capacity fails to achieve 25-30% utilization in year 1, unabsorbed depreciation (from ₹5,200 Cr capitalization) will compress consolidated PAT. Chinese LFP cell dumping is the primary external threat.

Aligned with management's guided 15-16% top-line growth. Consistently 3.5% to 4.5% higher on top-line than traditional broker consensus due to projecting stronger stationary BESS demand from rooftop solar.

10. What is the market missing regarding Exide's thesis? +

The Stationary Storage Blind Spot: Analysts treat EESL as a pure EV mobility play. Prismatic LFP cells require much shorter validation cycles for stationary BESS than auto homologation, unlocking earlier capacity utilization in FY28.
