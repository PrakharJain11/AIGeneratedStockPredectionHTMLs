Revenue Mix Shift & Margin Expansion Engine

Sansera's financial inflection is driven by a intentional structural pivot from traditional internal combustion engine (ICE) automotive forging to high-margin Aerospace, Defence, and Semiconductor (ADS) components and Tech-Agnostic/xEV products. This section details the segment transition trajectories, gross margin expansion drivers, and interest cost elimination dynamics.

Segment Mix Transition Trajectory

Dilution of lower-margin ICE vs rapid scaling of 30%+ margin ADS

Core Profitability & Margin Expansion Mechanics

1. High-Margin ADS Ramp-up 30%+ EBITDA Margin

ADS revenue tripled YoY in Q1 FY27 to ₹199.8 Cr (21% of sales). Supported by an executable order backlog of ₹4,464 Cr over 5 years and management's ₹550-600 Cr target for FY27.

2. Surface Treatment Internalization +70 to +90 bps Margin

The new NADCAP-certified surface treatment facility in Bengaluru (commissioning Q3 FY27) eliminates external processing leaks and keeps specialized value-add entirely in-house.

3. QIP Debt Deleveraging ₹650 Cr Debt Extinguished

The ₹1,200 Cr QIP in Q3 FY25 eliminated ~₹40 Cr in annual interest expense drag, transitioning Sansera to a net-cash position and creating a permanent multiplier for PAT growth over EBITDA.
