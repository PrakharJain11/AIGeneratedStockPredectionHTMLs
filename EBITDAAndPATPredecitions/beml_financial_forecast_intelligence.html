Margin Expansion & Cost Pass-Through

How BEML structurally protects gross margins and generates exponential bottom-line operating leverage.

✓ Price Variation Clauses (PVC) Chapter 31 Manual

Contracts with Indian Railways and Ministry of Defence embed strict Material Escalation Clauses, passing raw material commodity inflation (steel, alloy) directly to sovereign clients.

✓ Operating Leverage Mechanics Fixed Cost Absorption

Once revenue surpasses ~₹1,200 Cr in Q3/Q4 quarters, depreciation and fixed overhead costs are fully absorbed, causing incremental EBITDA to drop directly to net profit.
